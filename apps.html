Pet: Roger

A desktop pet you can pet, feed and play with. Roger, the judgemental fox demon, roams around your screen and keeps his opinions to himself (he doesn't).

  • Feed him cheese, steak, candy corn or a mysterious heart
  • Pet him, brush his fur, toss him a ball to chase
  • Put him to sleep, let him sit or wander, check his stats
  • Drag him around; double-click for a quick pat. Right-click for the menu
⬇ Download for Windows

Windows only (64-bit). Unzip, then run RogerPet.exe. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the app isn't signed: click "More info" then "Run anyway". About 95 MB.
