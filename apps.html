Pet: Roger

A desktop pet you can feed, pet, brush, play ball with, and grab. Roger, the judgemental fox demon, roams around your screen and keeps his opinions to himself (he doesn't).

  • Feed him cheese, steak, candy corn or a mysterious heart
  • Pet him, brush his fur, toss him a ball to chase
  • Put him to sleep, let him sit or wander, check his stats
  • Drag to pick him up by the scruff; click or double-click for a quick reaction. Right-click for the menu
⬇ Download for Windows ⬇ Mac (Apple Silicon) ⬇ Mac (Intel)

Windows (64-bit) and Mac · about 85–100 MB. Windows: unzip, then run RogerPet.exe. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the app isn't signed: click "More info" then "Run anyway".

Windows blocked it?
  1. If your browser or Defender flags the download, choose Keep / Keep anyway.
  2. Right-click the zip > Properties > tick Unblock > OK, then Extract All.
  3. "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen): click More info > Run anyway.
  4. If Defender quarantines it: Windows Security > Virus & threat protection > Protection history > select the item > Actions > Allow on device. Optional: Manage settings > Exclusions > Add an exclusion > Folder > pick the RogerPet folder.
Mac blocked it?

Mac builds are unsigned and untested, so macOS may say "Malware Blocked" or that the app is damaged.

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Apple Silicon: open Terminal in that folder and run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine RogerPet.app
  3. Then run codesign --force --deep --sign - RogerPet.app and open him.
  4. Intel: right-click the app > Open > Open.

* Roger isn't signed by Apple or Microsoft, so your computer may flag him as a threat (on Mac: "Malware Blocked"). He's safe, just unsigned.
